# 01: Facilitator Can Participate as Estimator or Observer

**Category:** `bug`
**Status:** `ready-for-agent`
**Blocked by:** none

## Problem Statement

Currently, Facilitators are either treated solely as non-voting observers or their role is conflated with Facilitator authority. In planning poker sessions, a Facilitator (e.g. Lead Engineer or Tech Lead) often needs to actively vote as an **Estimator** while maintaining facilitator controls (Start Voting, Reveal, Finalize). Alternatively, a Scrum Master or Product Manager may want to act as an **Observer** (non-voting facilitator).

## Verified Codebase Behavior

1. In `server/src/room/room-actor.ts`, the room creator is assigned `role: 'Facilitator'`, but client types and `PokerTableArena.tsx` filter by `p.role === 'Estimator'` when calculating quorum (`totalEstimators` and `votedCount`).
2. In `client/src/components/JoinModal.tsx`, users can only pick `Estimator` or `Observer`. When a user with facilitator authority chooses `Estimator`, their voting deck must be enabled and their vote must be included in consensus and quorum.
3. In `client/src/views/RoomView.tsx`, the `DeckSelector` is shown if `role !== 'Observer'`. If a Facilitator is in `Estimator` mode, they must be able to cast votes and flip their card on reveal.

## Agent Brief & Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Role & Authority Separation**: In `@scrumpokr/shared`, a participant has a participation role (`'Estimator' | 'Observer'`), while room facilitator authority is tracked via `facilitator_id` / `isFacilitator`.
- [ ] **Facilitator Voting**: A facilitator whose role is `Estimator` can cast votes via the `DeckSelector`, appears on the felt table as a voting participant with a crown badge (`👑`), and their vote counts towards quorum and consensus calculation.
- [ ] **Observer Facilitator**: A facilitator whose role is `Observer` does not have a voting deck, is displayed as an Observer on the table, and is excluded from voting quorum.
- [ ] **Role Switching**: Facilitators and participants can toggle their role between `Estimator` and `Observer` via the profile modal or header controls without losing facilitator authority.
- [ ] **Automated Tests**: Unit and integration tests covering voting facilitator vs. observer facilitator state transitions and consensus calculations.
